# Screenshots for v0.57.2 (Direct Reports table fits)

The screens that change between v0.57.1 and v0.57.2, with the fictional sample team in `tools/screenshots/sample-team.cjs`. All people and notes are fictional.

- `reports-table` is the standard screenshot at 1440 pixels wide, taken with `tools/screenshots/shoot.cjs`. In `before/` the table runs 16 pixels past its card and clips the Log meeting buttons. In `after/` it fits.
- `reports-table-1280` and `reports-table-1280-scrolled` show the table at 1280 pixels wide, as it opens and scrolled all the way right. In `before/` the names scroll out of view. In `after/` the Person column and the two buttons stay in place.

The other 64 standard screens are pixel for pixel identical, apart from the version number in the sidebar.
