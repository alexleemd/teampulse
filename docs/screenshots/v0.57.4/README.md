# Screenshots for v0.57.4 (Overview shows each problem once)

The screens that change between v0.57.3 and v0.57.4, taken with `tools/screenshots/shoot.cjs` and the fictional sample team in `tools/screenshots/sample-team.cjs`. All people and notes are fictional.

- `before/` shows v0.57.3. The Team Health card is stretched to the Briefing's height, the direct report tiles repeat the Briefing's problems as tags, and Upcoming sits at the bottom of the page.
- `after/` shows v0.57.4. The Team Health card ends at its content, Upcoming sits under it, and the tiles on the Overview leave out the problem tags. The tiles on Direct Reports keep them.

Search, the Data menu and the save conflict dialog change only because the Overview shows behind them. The other 57 screens are pixel for pixel identical, apart from the version number in the sidebar.
